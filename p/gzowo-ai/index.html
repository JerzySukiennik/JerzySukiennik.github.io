Gzowo AI

A voice assistant for the house in Gzowo — speak to it in Polish and it answers in real time.

Play Gzowo AI ↗ Source ↗

A Jarvis-style assistant with a black and white interface and a sphere for a face that shifts between idle, talking and showing. It listens and replies in Polish through a realtime audio model, remembers across sessions, and reaches into the house and the other projects here rather than being a chat window.

It runs on a Mac at home and costs nothing to operate. When the Mac sleeps the assistant degrades gently instead of breaking — the parts that need the machine go quiet and the rest keeps answering.
