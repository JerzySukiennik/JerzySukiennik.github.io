Modular

Drop sixteen kinds of building on a plot and watch a town assemble itself around them.

Play Modular ↗ Source ↗

A calm builder: you place buildings, they connect, and the place slowly turns into somewhere. Saves persist and you can build alongside someone else.

Most of the work went into the parts you do not notice — shadows that make the models sit on the ground properly, an audio bus so the ambience does not stack into noise, and a pixel ratio that drops itself on weaker machines instead of stuttering.
