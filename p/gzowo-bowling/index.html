Gzowo Bowling

Bowling on a laptop, thrown with a phone. Scan the code and your phone becomes the ball.

Play Gzowo Bowling ↗ Source ↗

The laptop shows the lane; your phone is the controller. You scan a code, and the swing of your arm is the throw — the motion sensors in the phone are the input, so nobody has to learn a control scheme.

Under the alley is a proper physics simulation with three.js and Rapier, built without a bundler: modules straight from a CDN, which is the pattern most of the games here still use.
