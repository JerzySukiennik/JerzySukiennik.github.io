The brand kit for Gzowo Space Program: the mark, the palette, the typefaces and the rules for putting them together, published as a single page with the files attached.

The look is deliberately severe — black ground, condensed capitals for headlines, a monospace for data, and nothing decorative. It is the identity the rocket site runs on.
