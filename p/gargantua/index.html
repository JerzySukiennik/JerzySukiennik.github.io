Gargantua

Co-op zero-gravity spacewalk next to a black hole. Push off carefully; there is no brake.

Play Gargantua ↗ Source ↗

You move by pushing off surfaces and firing short bursts, which means every mistake keeps travelling. Working together is less about coordination than about not sending your partner spinning.

The black hole is the reason for the whole thing: light bends around it, and the closer you drift the more the view stops behaving the way your eyes expect.
