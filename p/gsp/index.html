GSP

Gzowo Space Program — the site for the model rockets we actually launch.

Play GSP ↗

GSP is the name everything that flies goes out under. The site collects the rockets, the launches and the parts we print, and it is the closest thing the workshop has to a front door.

The rockets themselves are designed in OpenRocket, the printed parts in OpenSCAD, and they fly from the family plot in Gzowo — the same field the background of this page is made of.
