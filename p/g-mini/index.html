G-Mini

The second rung of the ladder: 178 million parameters, warm-started from G-Micro, trained in one week of free GPU time.

Not public yet Source ↗

The successor to G-Micro, aimed squarely at the things G-Micro gets wrong, with a hard budget: a single week of free Kaggle quota on two T4s. Every choice in the configuration came from a measurement rather than a guess.

Warm-starting from the smaller model rather than beginning at random is what makes the budget work — the new network inherits what the old one already learned about Polish and spends its week on what it does not know.
