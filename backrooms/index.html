Backrooms

Yellow rooms, buzzing lights, and a maze that does not care whether you get out.

Play Backrooms ↗ Source ↗

A first-person walk through an endless office that never quite repeats. There is no map and no marker; the only thing you build up is a sense of which corridor you have already ruined an hour on.

The tension is carried almost entirely by sound and by light that hums at the wrong frequency — there is very little in the way of enemies, and it does not need them.
