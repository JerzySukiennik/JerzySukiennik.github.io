G-Doodle

A transformer that draws. Type what you want in Polish and it sketches it stroke by stroke.

Not public yet Source ↗

The third model in the family, after G-Micro and G-Images, trained from scratch over 345 categories of drawing. It does not produce an image in one shot — it emits strokes in order, so you watch the picture being drawn rather than appear.

It is also the first of the family that needs nothing running at home. G-Micro and G-Images both go quiet when the Mac sleeps; this one is small enough to run in the browser it is displayed in.
